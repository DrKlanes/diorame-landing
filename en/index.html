Getting started

What is Diorame?

A free, browser-based illustration tool for drawing in 2D and bringing your work to life in 3D, instantly. Every layer sits at its own depth on its own, with parallax and cinematic camera moves. No 3D knowledge, no complex controls.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Completely free, no account, forever. No catch: open the app and start drawing. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.

Which devices and browsers does it work on?

Any modern desktop or tablet browser: Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge. Phones are not supported, by design: there just isn't room on such a small screen to make the most of it. Super Riso Lab does work on phones, although it's made for desktop.

Does it work with Apple Pencil or a stylus?

Yes. Diorame is optimized for tablets and works with any stylus your browser supports, Apple Pencil included. Touch gestures for zoom, pan, orbit and undo or redo work too.

Is it an alternative to Procreate, Illustrator or other drawing apps?

It doesn't try to replace Procreate, Fresco, Illustrator or Clip Studio Paint, which are professional tools with years of development behind them. Diorame is something else: an illustration tool built around simplicity, the riso look and automatic 3D depth. Think of it less as a competitor and more as a creative space you didn't have before.

What makes it different from other drawing apps?

Most drawing apps are flat. Diorame places every layer in 3D space, so when you switch to Cinema you get real parallax and cinematic camera moves, with no rigging and no manual positioning. It's the only free browser-based drawing tool built around the idea of a diorama.

Do I have to use the 3D and animation features?

Not at all. Diorame is a drawing app first. Blob, Brush, layers, palettes and modifiers are designed to make drawing simple and enjoyable on their own. 3D, cameras and effects are there when you want them.

Any tips before I start?

Save often. Diorame runs in the browser and, like any web app, it can close by accident; keep your .dior file at hand. And for the best video quality, besides the built-in MP4 exporter, you can use your device's own screen recording.

Drawing and animation

Which drawing tools does it have?

Two main ones: Blob, for filled, organic shapes, and Brush, for flowing, uniform or ink strokes. Plus an eraser, a text tool with 5 custom fonts and a tool to move whole layers. Modifiers include symmetry, draw inside, draw behind and organic mode.

Can I animate?

Yes. Diorame turns your layers into frames: a flipbook built on the same layer system you already use. Turn on animation in Draw, set the frame rate and play it as a loop or ping-pong. Onion skinning shows the neighbouring frames while you draw.

Do I need to know how to animate?

No. If you can draw in layers, you can animate. No timelines, no keyframes and no rigging: draw each frame and press play.

Which formats can I export animations to?

MP4 or WebM video with configurable loops, animated GIF with adjustable scale and loop or ping-pong, or a numbered PNG sequence to put together in After Effects or other software.

Does it have sound?

Yes, optional sound effects: small sounds that react to what you do, for a more immersive session. They're off by default and you can turn them on whenever you like.

What is a .dior file and how do I save my projects?

It's Diorame's project format: a single file with a thumbnail that holds your whole drawing. There's Save, Save as and Duplicate. In Chrome and Edge, Save overwrites the file; in Safari and Firefox, saving means downloading. With Diorame installed as an app, double-clicking a .dior opens it straight away.

Can I export my illustrations?

Yes: PNG, SVG, SVGZ and WebM or MP4 video with the camera in motion. High-quality export redraws every stroke and every effect at twice the resolution; it isn't an upscale, so the result is genuinely sharp.

Cinema

What are the camera moves?

Eleven: Forward, Spiral, Yoyo, Pulse, Twist, Arc, Crane, Truck, Orbit, Zoom and Storytelling. They all use the real depth of your layers. You can add a handheld feel at three intensities and adjust the overall speed.

What is the Storytelling camera?

A camera move that animates your drawing by itself. Instead of a fixed path, it reads your layers and composes a dolly shot that travels through them from back to front, pausing on each one. Every drawing gets its own route. With depth of field on, focus travels with the camera. One click, zero setup.

Which effects are included?

Thirteen: Super Riso, riso halftone, pixel art, depth of field, film grain, chromatic aberration, fog, glow, vignette, lens distortion, particles, grunge and line wiggle. All adjustable and combinable.

What is Pixel Art mode?

I love pixel art, so it had to be there: it turns your illustration into pixel art in real time, with pixel size from 2 to 16, 2 to 32 colours and adjustable Bayer dithering. It combines with every other effect.

Does stacking effects affect performance?

It can. Combining many effects on a modest machine may slow the camera down in Cinema. If you notice it, reduce the number of active effects or their intensity. Draw always runs smoothly, whatever effects you've set.

Super Riso and printing

What is Super Riso?

A Cinema effect that makes your scene look hand-printed, in real time, with three techniques: Riso, Photocopy and Stencil. It's applied to every frame while the camera moves, so it works with your animations too. They share one-click styles, print settings, animated reprinting from 4 to 12 fps and full-quality export. On iPad it lightens itself automatically.

Can I make risograph-style illustration?

Yes. Diorame was born from the riso look: fixed palettes, procedural halftone and registration effects. For a richer finish, Super Riso turns your scene into a scanned risograph, with up to 8 inks, grain or dot screens, misregistration and its own paper textures.

Can I make photocopy or copy art effects?

Yes, with Super Riso's Photocopy technique, punk and zine style: toner darkness, copy-of-a-copy generations, real grime (specks, hairs, scratches, drum streaks), line or photo mode, fluorescent papers and hand colouring over the copy with marker or watercolour.

Can I make stencil and spray paint art?

Yes, with the Stencil technique: 1 to 4 stencils by tone or by your drawing's colours, cut bridges, spray speckle, lifted stencil, drips, splatters and a background cloud, on wall, dark wall, cardboard, canvas, wood or paper. Drips and splatters appear when the camera is still, by design.

What is Super Riso Lab?

Diorame's print studio: free, in your browser and with no account. Open any image or a .dior project, apply Riso, Photocopy or Stencil, choose from seven papers and export. With a .dior you frame your drawing with zoom and pan, and everything is redrawn as vectors. From Draw you get there with Export › Super Riso, and come back with ‹ Diorame.

Can I really print my drawings?

Yes. Super Riso Lab exports sheets from A6 to A2 at up to 600 dpi, print runs of variations, a video loop and one plate per ink, ready for a real riso. If you just want your drawing as it is, “No effect” exports it in high resolution.

About the project

What inspired Diorame?

Graintouch, a beautiful iPad drawing app by Iorama Studio: its riso look, its simplicity and its organic tools. Diorame's core idea is its own: automatic 3D depth per layer, real parallax, cinematic cameras and real-time printing.

Diorame was built with AI. What do you think about that?

I'm an illustrator, designer and drawer, not a programmer. For years I believed the drawing app in my head would stay out of reach. AI changed that, not as a substitute for creativity or talent (I reject that idea completely), but as a collaborator that let me build something I couldn't have built alone.

The code is mine in the sense that matters: every decision, every feature and every constraint is intentional and comes from years of thinking about what my ideal drawing app should be. The AI wrote the syntax. I kept the vision.
