Home / Parallax illustration

Parallax for illustrators. No After Effects. No suffering.

You draw in layers. Diorame puts each one at its own depth and drags a camera through them, and your flat illustration becomes a scene with actual parallax. No timeline. No keyframes. No rigging. Free, no account, nothing to install. Suspiciously simple, we know.

Open Diorame

What parallax is (and why it works)

Look out of a moving train. The fence flies by, the mountains barely move. Your brain reads that as depth. Cut an illustration into layers, put each one at a different distance and you can fake it on a flat drawing. Cheap trick, works every time.

How Diorame does it

Every layer lives in its own place in space. You draw flat, the camera makes the depth. There is nothing to position by hand, which is the whole point.

From drawing to video in four steps

  1. Draw each plane on its own layer: background, subject and foreground. Three planes are enough to feel the depth. More is showing off.
  2. Open Cinema and pick a camera move. There are 11: Forward, Orbit, Crane, Spiral and company, plus an optional handheld feel at three intensities, for when you want it to look like a person filmed it. Storytelling builds a dolly shot from the back layer to the front one in a single click.
  3. Throw effects on it if you feel like it: depth of field, fog, film grain, glow, vignette and a few more, stackable.
  4. Export as MP4, WebM, GIF or a PNG sequence. Done.

What it does not do

Diorame is not After Effects and has no intention of becoming it. No timeline, no keyframes, no rigging, and the camera moves are presets with adjustable speed, not paths you animate by hand. Need a character walking or a custom motion path? Go and suffer with the right tool. Want your illustration to move in depth without a learning curve? You are in the right place.

Questions

Do I need 3D skills?

No. You draw in 2D and the camera builds the depth. The third dimension is somebody else's problem.

Is it free?

Yes. Free forever, no account, nothing to install. It is open source (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). No catch, no trial, no "upgrade to Pro".

What can I export?

MP4 or WebM with configurable loops, GIF with adjustable scale, or a numbered PNG sequence.

Does it replace After Effects?

No. It does one thing, parallax and camera moves on layered illustrations, and does it without a timeline. For everything else, After Effects is still there.
